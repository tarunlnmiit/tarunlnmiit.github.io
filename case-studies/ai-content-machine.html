AI Content Machine

A full-stack content factory that publishes 200+ pieces a month across 7 platforms for 3 distinct niches — run by 67 Python scripts, launchd schedulers, and one operator dashboard.

01Why it exists

Consistent multi-platform publishing is a full-time job: research, drafting, design, video, scheduling, repurposing, analytics. Doing it for three niches (Data Science · Life · Poetry) manually is impossible. The machine treats content as a pipeline problem: every stage scripted, scheduled, and measured.

02How it works

  1. ResearchRSS feeds, Google Suggest, and YouTube/Instagram trend scrapers surface what is worth covering.
  2. Ideation in NotionIdeas land in a Notion pipeline with status tracking — the single source of truth for what is in flight.
  3. Generation with Claude67 Python scripts ghostwrite blogs, video scripts, slides, thumbnails, and carousels per niche voice.
  4. Video productionRemotion renders videos programmatically; FFmpeg auto-edits; B-roll generated for Reels and Shorts.
  5. Publish & learnBuffer schedules everything across YouTube, LinkedIn, Medium, Instagram, Twitter, Substack, and podcasts. A closed-loop analytics pipeline feeds performance back into topic selection.
