Content-Machine Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Content-Machine is a personal automation tool built and operated by Tarun Gupta for his own use. It is not offered to the public, and only its owner signs in to it.

What it accesses

With the owner's explicit consent through Google sign-in, the tool accesses the owner's own YouTube channel to upload videos, edit video titles and descriptions, and read channel and video analytics.

What it stores

The sign-in token that lets the tool act on the owner's channel is stored on the owner's own computer. Nothing is collected from, or stored about, any other person.

Sharing

Data from Google APIs is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
